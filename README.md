# Artūrs Jānis Ņikitins

**MSc Economic and Social Sciences Student @ Bocconi | Former Senior Economist @ Latvijas Banka**

I am interested in empirical macro-finance and applied macroeconomics, with a focus on monetary policy transmission, banking, forecasting and granular financial data.

### Research

* [Household Borrowing and Monetary Policy Transmission: Post-Pandemic Insights from Nine European Credit Registers](https://www.ijcb.org/journal/v22n2/household-borrowing-and-monetary-policy-transmission-post-pandemic-insights-nine) — *International Journal of Central Banking* (2026), co-author.
* **Current research:** bank-level heterogeneity in monetary-policy transmission and macroeconomic expectations using US bank data and local projections, in a Bocconi IGIER project with Filippo De Marco and Nicola Pavoni.

### Selected projects

* [Macroeconomic forecasting](https://github.com/ajnikitins/australia-forecasting) — forecasting of Australian GDP growth using AR, VAR, ARDL, ARMA and ARMAX models.
* [State-space modelling](https://github.com/ajnikitins/temperature-state-space) — HMMs and dynamic linear models with Kalman filtering/smoothing, spatial covariance modelling and forecasting.
* [BSc thesis](https://github.com/ajnikitins/bsc-thesis) — large-scale empirical project combining transaction, conflict, news and social-media data with NLP/transformer classification and econometric analysis.
* [r.loaders](https://github.com/ajnikitins/r.loaders) — R utilities for retrieving and caching ECB Data Portal data and connecting programmatically to SQL Server.
